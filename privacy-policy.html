How we handle your data.

  • We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

  • A short, named list of tools touches your data — CRM, hosting, payments — never an open-ended list of "partners."
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete what we hold — email brighterdaysolution@gmail.com.

01

Who this covers

This policy is issued by Brighter Day Solutions LLC ("BDS," "we," "us"), and covers the sites and services we operate directly, plus services delivered through our marketing agency, The Brighter Day Company ("TBDC"), and our white-labeled CRM/marketing-automation portal at portal.brighterdayagency.com.

If you're a client running your own sub-account on our portal, this policy governs how BDS itself handles data as the platform operator. If you collect data from your own customers through your sub-account, you're the one responsible for how you use it and for having your own privacy notice for them.

03

How we use it

  • Operate and support the CRM/marketing portal and your account inside it
  • Respond to inquiries and deliver the marketing, website, or automation services you've engaged us for
  • Send service updates, billing notices, and — where you've opted in — marketing emails and texts
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations
  • Improve our own services based on aggregate usage patterns

04

Texting & calls

If you opt in to SMS or voice communications through our portal or booking flow, message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies based on your account activity. Consent to receive texts or calls is never a condition of purchasing any service.

  • Text STOP to any message to opt out at any time; text HELP for assistance.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Phone numbers collected for texting are not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

06

Cookies

Our website and portal use cookies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand how pages are used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings — some portal features may not work correctly without them.

07

Retention & security

We keep account and communication data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period after for legal, billing, and record-keeping purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
