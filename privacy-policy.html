Plain language, before the legal text

We collect the contact and business information you or your clients give us through our website, forms, and CRM/marketing portal, so we can run that portal, send the emails and texts you've asked for, and support your account.

  • We don't sell your data.
  • Texting is opt-in — reply STOP anytime to end it.
  • Your information passes through a short list of named tools below (CRM, hosting, payments) — never an open-ended list of "partners."
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete what we hold on you — write to brighterdaysolution@gmail.com.

01 Who this covers

This policy is issued by Brighter Day Solutions LLC ("BDS," "we," "us"), and covers the sites and services we operate directly, plus services delivered through our marketing agency, The Brighter Day Company ("TBDC"), and our white-labeled CRM/marketing-automation portal at portal.brighterdayagency.com.

If you're a client running your own sub-account on our portal, this policy governs how BDS itself handles data as the platform operator. If you collect data from your own customers through your sub-account, you're the one responsible for how you use it and for having your own privacy notice for them.

04 Texting & calls

If you opt in to SMS or voice communications through our portal or booking flow, message and data rates may apply and message frequency varies based on your account activity. Consent to receive texts or calls is never a condition of purchasing any service.

  • Text STOP to any message to opt out at any time; text HELP for assistance.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Phone numbers collected for texting are not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
